# Acme Inc Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance. Applies to all employees. All amounts are in USD.

## 1. Submission rules

1.1 A claim must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
1.2 Foreign-currency expenses are converted to USD at the rate on the expense date.
1.3 A receipt is required for any single expense over $25. Expenses of $25 or less may be self-declared.
1.4 A valid receipt shows the vendor, the date, itemized charges, the total, and the payment method.

## 2. Approval authority

2.1 Claims of $1,000 or less can be approved by the reviewer.
2.2 Claims over $1,000 need Finance Director sign-off. The reviewer may recommend approval but must flag the claim for sign-off.
2.3 A reviewer has three outcomes: approve (in full or with adjustments), reject, or request more information.

## 3. Travel

3.1 Business travel needs an approved travel request ID on the claim.
3.2 Air travel is economy class only and must be booked at least 7 days before departure. Business class needs written VP pre-approval.
3.3 Lodging is reimbursed up to $200 per night before taxes. Room tax is reimbursable. Minibar, in-room movies, spa, and personal laundry are not.
3.4 Taxis and rideshares are reimbursable for business travel. Tips are reimbursable up to 20% of the fare.
3.5 Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile with a trip log.

## 4. Meals

4.1 Travel meals: up to $75 per day in total, covering all meals that day, including tax and tip.
4.2 Client meals: up to $100 per person. The claim must list attendee names and the business purpose.
4.3 Team meals: up to $50 per person, including tax and tip. The claim must list attendees. Events with more than 10 attendees need manager pre-approval.
4.4 Alcohol is never reimbursable. Remove alcohol line items together with the tax and tip attributable to them, then apply the limits above to what remains.
4.5 Tips are reimbursable up to 20% of the pre-tax amount.

## 5. Work from home

5.1 Internet and phone: up to $50 per month combined. One claim per month.
5.2 Equipment: up to $300 per calendar year, receipt required.

## 6. Office supplies and software

6.1 Office supplies: up to $100 per claim.
6.2 Software subscriptions need written IT approval attached to the claim.

## 7. Learning and development

7.1 Each employee has a $1,500 learning budget per calendar year, covering courses, certifications, and conferences.
7.2 Any single learning expense over $500 needs the employee's manager's written pre-approval attached to the claim.

## 8. Health and wellness

8.1 Medical expenses (doctor visits, prescriptions, clinic fees) are not reimbursable. They are covered by the company health insurance plan.
8.2 A wellness allowance of $50 per month covers gym memberships, fitness classes, and meditation apps.

## 9. Duplicates and discrepancies

9.1 A receipt with the same vendor, date, amount, and receipt number as one already submitted or reimbursed is a duplicate. Reject it.
9.2 Recurring bills with different invoice numbers or service periods (for example, monthly internet) are not duplicates.
9.3 If the amount claimed differs from the receipt, reimburse the receipt amount and note the discrepancy.
9.4 If information needed for a decision is missing, request it from the employee. Do not guess.

## 10. Never reimbursable

Alcohol, personal items, fines and penalties, entertainment, and expenses for family members.
